Media

Audio and media, kept in one library

Recordings, photographs, and clips live in a Google Drive folder with two subfolders: Audio and Media. Open the library to listen, download, or share with a host.

Subfolder

Audio

Keynotes, interviews, and workshop excerpts. Useful for event pages and for hosts who want to hear the room before they book.

Subfolder

Media

Photographs, short video, and press stills. High-resolution files for programs, websites, and day-of screens.

Open the full library

Create a Drive folder named for your speaking work, add Audio and Media subfolders, share it with “anyone with the link,” then paste the URL in the two cards above and the button below.

Open Google Drive

  1. 01

    Create the folder

    In Google Drive, make a folder. Inside it, add two subfolders: Audio and Media.

  2. 02

    Share the link

    Right-click the parent folder → Share → Anyone with the link → Viewer. Copy the link.

  3. 03

    Paste it here

    Replace YOUR_FOLDER_ID in this file (three places) with your folder URL.
