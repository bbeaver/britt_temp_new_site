About

Eighteen years in a classroom. Now the work is the room itself.

Britt spent nearly two decades teaching public-school English before taking the same craft — presence, story, a well-kept last sentence — to keynotes, faculties, and leadership stages. The aim is not to perform. It is to make a room of people care about an idea, and leave able to use it.

The work

Three ways to bring Britt in

01

Keynotes

30–60 minutes. A full-room talk on presence, story, and the quiet authority of a well-chosen sentence.

02

Workshops

Half or full day. Hands-on sessions for faculties, leadership teams, and professional associations.

03

Coaching

Private or small group. Preparation for a specific stage: a TED-style talk, a commencement, a difficult all-hands.

Talks & workshops

Subjects a room can actually use

  1. 01

    Finding the room

    How to walk into a gymnasium, a boardroom, or a ballroom and hold attention without raising your voice.

  2. 02

    Stories that teach

    The classroom habit of making an idea stick — applied to keynotes, all-staff days, and conference stages.

  3. 03

    Courage on the first line

    Workshops for people who freeze at a microphone: teachers, new managers, and anyone asked to “just say a few words.”
